# Sony WH-1000XM5 Wireless Noise Canceling Headphones — Help Guide & Manual

## Product Overview & Specifications

The Sony WH-1000XM5 is a premium wireless over-ear headphone featuring industry-leading active noise cancellation powered by the Integrated Processor V1 and HD Noise Canceling Processor QN1.

### Specifications
- **Model**: WH-1000XM5
- **Bluetooth Version**: 5.2 (Supported Codecs: SBC, AAC, LDAC)
- **Battery Life**: Up to 30 hours with Active Noise Canceling (ANC) ON, up to 40 hours with ANC OFF
- **Quick Charge**: 3 minutes of USB-PD charging yields up to 3 hours of playback
- **Microphones**: 8 microphones total (4 on each earcup) with Beamforming and AI-based noise reduction
- **Weight**: Approx. 250 g
- **Wearing Detection**: Capacitive proximity sensor inside the left earcup

## Bluetooth Pairing

### Initial Device Pairing (First Time)
1. Turn on the headphones by pressing and holding the **Power button** for about 2 seconds until the blue LED indicator flashes.
2. The headphones automatically enter Bluetooth pairing mode upon first power-on.
3. Open Bluetooth settings on your smartphone or computer and select **"WH-1000XM5"**.

### Pairing with a Second Device (Multipoint Connection)
1. Ensure the headphones are turned on.
2. Press and hold the **Power button** for approximately **7 seconds** until you hear the voice prompt saying *"Bluetooth pairing"* and the blue indicator flashes rapidly in pairs.
3. In the **Sony | Headphones Connect** app, go to the **System** tab and toggle ON **"Connect to 2 devices simultaneously"**.
4. Select the second device from your Bluetooth list. You can seamlessly switch playback between both connected devices.

## Factory Reset & Initialization

### Resetting the Headphones (Preserves Pairing Information)
If the headset does not operate correctly or will not turn on:
1. Connect the headset to a powered USB-C port using the supplied charging cable.
2. Press the **Power button** and the **NC/AMB (Noise Canceling / Ambient Sound) button** simultaneously.
3. The headset is reset. Device registration (pairing) information is retained.

### Initializing the Headphones (Restores Factory Default Settings)
To erase all pairing information and restore factory settings:
1. Turn off the headset and disconnect the USB-C cable.
2. Press and hold both the **Power button** and the **NC/AMB button** simultaneously for at least **7 seconds**.
3. The blue indicator will flash **4 times**, and the headset will be initialized to factory settings. All paired device data will be erased.

## Touch Sensor Control Panel (Right Earcup)

- **Play / Pause**: Double-tap the center of the right touch sensor panel.
- **Next Track**: Swipe forward horizontally.
- **Previous Track**: Swipe backward horizontally.
- **Volume Up**: Swipe up repeatedly or swipe up and hold.
- **Volume Down**: Swipe down repeatedly or swipe down and hold.
- **Quick Attention Mode**: Cover the entire right touch sensor panel with your hand to temporarily lower audio volume and amplify ambient sound.
- **Voice Assistant**: Touch and hold the center of the panel for 2 seconds.

## Troubleshooting & FAQ

### Issue 1: Noise Canceling Effect Seems Weak
1. Ensure the ear cushions form a complete seal around your ears without hair or glasses frames breaking the seal.
2. Open the **Sony | Headphones Connect** app and run the **NC Optimizer** to calibrate for atmospheric pressure and wearing conditions.
3. Turn off **Adaptive Sound Control** if the headset is automatically switching between Ambient Sound and Noise Canceling based on your location or activity.

### Issue 2: Intermittent Sound Dropouts / Stuttering
1. Open the Sony Headphones Connect app and set **Bluetooth Connection Quality** to **"Priority on Stable Connection"** instead of "Priority on Sound Quality".
2. Keep the headset clear of strong 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi routers or active microwave ovens.
3. Ensure no physical obstruction exists between the device antenna and the left headphone earcup.

### Issue 3: Speak-to-Chat Activates Unexpectedly
When Speak-to-Chat is enabled, audio pauses automatically when you speak. If you cough or hum:
1. Hold two fingers against the right touch sensor panel until you hear *"Speak-to-chat deactivated"*, or adjust the detection sensitivity in the Sony Headphones Connect app.
